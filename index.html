✈️ XATRUCH JOY BOMBER

🕹️ Controls

  • SPACE or click: Drop / Jump (Double jump on Highway)
  • 1 2 3 4 5: Direct supply selection
  • Q / E: Cycle carousel
  • A / D: Adjust speed

🎯 Compatibility

  • 🏘️ Low-Income Belt ➔ 💵 Money, 💧 Water or 🥖 Food
  • 🏙️ Civic Center / Modern ➔ 📖 Bible / Wisdom
  • 🏡 Hillside Neighborhood ➔ 🎁 Gifts, 🥖 Food or 💵 Money
  • 🔥 Active Wildfire ➔ 💧 Water Bomb

💖 Mission of Joy

Calculate the plane's parabolic inertia when dropping supplies. Keep the Joy-O-Meter high and chain hits to multiply combos (2x, 3x, 4x).
